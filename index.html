Support

SMOKEY HQ is the private team workspace for SMOKEY (DJ, producer and vocalist) and his management, booking and marketing team.

Contact

Email smokeydubzz@gmail.com. We reply within 2 business days.

Getting access

Team members are invited by the owner. Open SMOKEY HQ, tap Have an invite code?, and enter your email and the 6-digit code from your invite email. If you created an account yourself, the owner assigns your role before team data appears.

Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?, enter your email, then enter the 6-digit code we send you and choose a new password.

Delete your account

In the app open More and tap Delete my account. This permanently removes your login and profile. You can also email us to request deletion.
