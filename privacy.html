SMOKEY HQ

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

SMOKEY HQ ("the app") is a private workspace used by SMOKEY and his team to manage contacts, bookings, releases, tasks, documents and budgets. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used. Questions: smokeydubzz@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash) when you create an account or accept an invite.
  • Content you and your team enter: industry contacts, notes and conversation history, bookings and offers, releases, tasks, documents you upload, and budget entries.
  • Basic technical logs: our hosting provider records standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) to keep the service secure and working.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not collect location, contacts from your phone, health or payment data.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, confirm your email, reset your password and send team invitations.
  • To show your team the shared information your role allows (owner, management, booking or marketing).

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication and file storage, hosted in the United States). Account emails are delivered by our email provider. These providers process data only to run the service.

Retention and deletion

Your account stays active until you delete it. In the app open More → Delete my account to permanently delete your login and profile, or email us. Business records you added for the team (for example a contact or task) remain with the team without your name attached.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted by role on the server.

Children

The app is for business use by adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We'll update this page if this policy changes and change the effective date above.
